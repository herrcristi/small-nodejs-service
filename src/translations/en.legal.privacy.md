# Privacy Policy

Effective date: {effectiveDate}

This Privacy Policy explains how the personal data of the users of the school management application available at {appUrl} (the "Application") is processed, in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR") and the applicable national law.

## 1. Who is responsible for your data

{operatorName}, {operatorAddress}, contact {operatorEmail} ("we", "us"), is the controller of the data of your account: your login, your profile, your sessions, the records of your acceptance of our documents and the security of the Application.

Your school is the controller of the data that it manages about its members in the Application (its students, professors and administrators, its classes, groups and schedules). We process that data on behalf of your school, as its processor, under a data processing agreement with it. For questions about that data, contact the administrators of your school first; we will help them answer you.

## 2. What data we process

- Account data: your email address (also your login), your name, your preferred language and the status of your account.
- Your password: when the Application manages the passwords, only a salted hash of it is stored, never the password itself. When an authentication provider is configured, the password is managed by that provider.
- Profile data that you choose to add: your birthday, your phone number and your address.
- School data: the schools of which you are a member, your roles in them, and the records of the school about you (for example your classes, groups and schedules).
- Records of acceptance: the versions of the End User License Agreement, the Terms and Conditions and this Privacy Policy that you accepted, and when.
- Technical and security data: your sessions (when they started and were last used, when they expire), the IP address of your requests (used to limit abusive requests and recorded in the server logs), and a log of the actions done in the Application (who did what and when), kept for security and accountability.
- Emails that we send you: invitations, account setup and password reset messages.

We do not use your data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not make decisions about you based only on automated processing, including profiling.

## 3. Why we process it and on what legal basis

- To create and run your account and to provide the Application to you and to your school: the performance of the contract with you (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR), and, for the data of the school, the instructions of your school.
- To keep the Application and your data secure, to prevent abuse and to investigate incidents: our legitimate interest in the security of the Application and of its users (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).
- To keep the records of your acceptance of our documents: our legitimate interest in being able to prove which terms apply to you (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).
- To comply with legal obligations, for example the requests of the authorities: Article 6(1)(c) GDPR.

The data of your profile that is optional (birthday, phone number, address) is processed only if you, or your school, enter it.

## 4. Cookies and storage in your browser

The Application uses a single cookie: the session cookie, which keeps you logged in. It is strictly necessary, it cannot be read by the scripts of the page, and it ends with your session. The Application also keeps in your browser your chosen language and the school that you selected, so that they are kept when the page is reloaded. We use no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

## 5. Who receives your data

- The members of your school, according to their roles (see the Terms and Conditions).
- Our service providers, as our processors and only to provide the Application: the hosting of the servers and of the database, the delivery of the emails and, if it is configured, the authentication provider. They are bound by contracts that protect your data.
- Authorities, when the law requires it.

If a provider processes data outside the European Economic Area, we make sure that the transfer is protected by an adequacy decision of the European Commission or by Standard Contractual Clauses. The list of our providers is available on request.

## 6. How long we keep your data

- Your account data and your records of acceptance: as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, they are deleted, except what we must keep by law.
- An invitation that is not used: it is removed automatically when it expires (72 hours by default).
- Sessions: they end after at most 12 hours, or after 30 minutes without activity, and their records are removed automatically. A password reset link is valid for 1 hour, an account setup link for 72 hours.
- The records of a school about you: as long as the school keeps them. When you leave a school or delete your account, the school decides, by its settings, if your records are kept as a trace or deleted.
- The log of actions and the server logs: for the limited period needed for security and accountability.

## 7. Your rights

You have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to its processing, and to receive the data that you provided in a structured, machine-readable format (Articles 15 to 21 GDPR). Where a processing is based on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time, without affecting the processing done before.

You can change your profile and delete your account directly in the Application. For the other requests, contact us at {operatorEmail}. We answer within one month; we may need to confirm your identity first.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the member state of your residence, of your place of work or of the alleged infringement. In Romania, this is the National Supervisory Authority for Personal Data Processing (ANSPDCP, www.dataprotection.ro).

## 8. Children

The Application can be used by students who are minors. Their data is entered and managed by their school, which is responsible for having a lawful basis for it, including the consent of a parent or legal guardian where the law requires it. A student who is under the age at which they can accept our documents alone must accept them together with a parent or legal guardian.

## 9. Security

We protect your data with appropriate technical and organisational measures: encrypted connections, passwords stored only as salted hashes, sessions that expire, access by roles (each member sees only what concerns them), limits on the number of requests, and the separation of the data of each school. No system is completely secure; if a breach of your data puts your rights at high risk, we will inform you without undue delay.

## 10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may change this Privacy Policy. The new version is shown to you in the Application, with its effective date, and you are asked to accept it to continue using the Application.
